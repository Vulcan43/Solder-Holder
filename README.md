# Why I made it 
I made this because I always have my solder rolling around or on one of those cheap holders that are so light that they fall or break. So I made this so for other people that need a solder holder they can 3D print it for cheaper. This holds your Solder, flux and Soldering Iron cleaner.

# What it is 
It is a 3d printable part that holds your Solder, flux and Soldering Iron cleaner. This is ment to take very little room but still be helpful.
